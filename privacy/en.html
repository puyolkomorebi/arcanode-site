ARCANODE Demo Support Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This privacy policy describes how the game app "ARCANODE" (the "App") and its demo handle user information.

Information we collect

The App does not collect any personal information or other data. It does not use the network to play.

Where data is stored

Your progress in the App (completed requests, stars, board layouts, chosen language, sound settings, and so on) is stored on your device. It is never sent to the developer's or any third party's servers.

You can erase your progress with Settings > Delete progress. It is also removed from your device when you delete the App. Your data may be included in device backups (such as iCloud Backup), which are handled under Apple's privacy policy.

In the demo you can play in a PC web browser (published on unityroom), progress is stored in that browser. Information about visits to the demo page is handled under unityroom's terms of service and privacy policy.

Purchase

The App is purchased through Apple's App Store. Payment information is handled by Apple, and the developer never receives it. The App has no in-app purchases.

Third parties

The App does not use any services or libraries for advertising, analytics, or tracking. We never share user information with third parties.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please use the support contact listed on the App's App Store page.

Changes

We may update this policy as needed. When we do, we will post the changes on this page with the date of the last update.
